How this list was built

Each restaurant was found through web research across neighbourhood guides, business-association directories, review sites and local news, then checked one by one: it must sit inside Kitchener, Waterloo or Cambridge city limits, serve real meals, be open as of October 2026, have fewer than 10 locations anywhere, and cost under $40 a person for a main and a non-alcoholic drink before tax and tip.

Before you go

Prices are typical estimates and menus change. Restaurants open and close often, so check hours on the restaurant's own site or map listing before heading out.
